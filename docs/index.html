Common questions

The daily notification isn't arriving. Open Settings inside the app and confirm "Send a daily notification" is on. Then check iOS Settings → Notifications → TodayIs and make sure alerts are allowed.

What is the 18+ tab? An optional list of adult-humor observances. It's off by default and only appears after you confirm your age in the app's Settings. You can turn it back off anytime.

Some dates feel arbitrary. National days come from many sources and aren't official. Dates are best-effort; email corrections and they'll go in an update.
