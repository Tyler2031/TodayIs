Privacy Policy

Effective September 1, 2026

TodayIs does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. All app data and settings remain on your device. The app makes no network requests.

What the app stores on your device

Only your own preferences — whether the daily notification is on, the time you chose, the featured category, and whether the 18+ tab is unlocked. This is kept in the device's standard local settings storage and never leaves the device. Deleting the app removes it.

Notifications

The daily reminder is a local notification scheduled on your device. No server is involved and nothing about your usage is sent anywhere.

No analytics, no tracking, no third parties

TodayIs contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising, and no third-party services. It does not use the Advertising Identifier and does not track you across apps or websites.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children. The optional 18+ humor section is disabled by default and gated behind an in-app age confirmation.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
